Privacy Policy

App: Ten31
Last updated: 2026

Summary: this app collects nothing. Ten31 has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and requires no network connection. Everything runs entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. The app has no login, no user profiles, and no server component.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best round and a flag remembering that you saw the one-time setup tip. This stays on your device (in local storage) and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Network use

Ten31 works fully offline. All visuals, voices, and sounds are bundled in the app; no requests are made to any server.

Children

Ten31 is a horror game rated 17+ and is not directed at children.
