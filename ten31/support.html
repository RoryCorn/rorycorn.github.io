Common questions

The screen slides down when I swipe near the bottom. That's iOS Reachability. Turn it off in Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Reachability for the smoothest play.

I don't hear the voices. Turn your volume up while in the game. Ten31 plays audio even with the silent switch on.

Does the app collect my data? No. Ten31 collects nothing and works fully offline. See our Privacy Policy.
