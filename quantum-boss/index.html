How it plays

Your eyes are the weapon.

  1. 01

    01 Look

    Aim by looking. Your front camera follows your head as you look up and down, right on your device. Touch works too.

  2. 02

    02 Hold

    Hold your gaze on a band. HIGH ORBIT, MID or ATMOSPHERE. The reticle locks.

  3. 03

    03 Collapse

    The hull nearest the planet collapses. Chain collapses for a combo multiplier up to ×8.

Let a hull reach the surface and Planetary Integrity drops.

At zero, the run ends.

Requirements and privacy

Plays right in your browser.Nothing leaves your device.

Requirements

A phone, iPad or computer with a current browser, such as Safari or Chrome.

Head tracking uses the front camera. Allow camera access when asked, or play by touch instead.

When you start, it asks you to look up, then down, to fit the bands to you. If your movement is small, it uses two bigger bands instead of three.

Works best in a well-lit room. The original iPhone edition, played with the Face ID camera, is not released.

Privacy

No account. No sign-in. No ads. No analytics.

The camera picture is processed live on your device. It is never recorded, stored, or uploaded.

The face-tracking software, Google's open-source MediaPipe, downloads when you start head tracking. Nothing from your camera is sent anywhere.

Your best score is kept only in your browser.
