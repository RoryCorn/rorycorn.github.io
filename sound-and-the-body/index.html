Two kinds of number, never mixed

Search "healing frequencies" and you get a wall of confident claims with nothing behind them. Search the real literature and you get EEG bands, NMR resonances and atomic transitions with no way to feel them. This atlas holds both, and keeps a hard line between them.

  • Measured science. EEG brainwave bands, NMR and MRI resonances, motor-unit firing rates, the caesium atomic clock. Each one carries a published citation you can open.
  • Traditional, unproven. Solfeggio tones, chakra pitches, the "528 Hz DNA repair" tone. Included because people look for them — and labelled, every time, as belief rather than evidence.

One switch flips the whole atlas between the two, so you always know which world you are standing in.

You can hear all of it

Tones are generated live in the browser, on your device. Most of the real physical frequencies here — megahertz to terahertz — are far above hearing, so the atlas plays a clearly-labelled audible stand-in and says so on screen rather than pretending. Anything below about 40 Hz is played as an isochronic pulse, so you hear the rhythm instead of silence.

Start at a low volume, especially with headphones. If you have a seizure disorder, be aware that rhythmic pulsing sound can be a trigger.

What it does with your data: almost nothing

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers. The atlas itself runs entirely in your browser — every diagram, every 3D model and every tone is generated on your device, and nothing about what you look at or listen to is sent anywhere.

The one exception is buying it. A purchase can't happen offline, so checkout and the unlock code go through Stripe and this site's own small server. That is the only network connection involved, and it carries nothing about your use of the atlas.
