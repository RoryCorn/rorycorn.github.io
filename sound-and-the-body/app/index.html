    This app is for education, curiosity and creative exploration only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.     The app separates two very different things: measured physical frequencies (EEG brainwave bands, NMR/MRI resonances, molecular terahertz vibrations, the cesium atomic clock) are real science; "healing" frequency claims (Solfeggio, chakra tuning, Rife frequencies, "528 Hz DNA repair") are traditional or speculative beliefs that are not supported by scientific evidence.     🔒 Privacy

    This app is built to respect your privacy completely:

    • No accounts or sign-in.
    • No analytics, ads, trackers, or cookies.
    • The atlas works offline. Every diagram, 3D model and tone is generated on your device. Nothing about what you look at or listen to is recorded or sent anywhere.
    • Saved on your device only: that you accepted this notice, when your trial began, and your settings.

    The one exception: purchases

    Unlocking the app is a purchase, and purchases cannot happen offline. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore, it contacts Apple and RevenueCat, the service that verifies the purchase. They receive an anonymous purchase identifier and receipt details from Apple — not your name, and nothing about your use of the atlas.

    That is the only network connection the app makes. We have no server of our own, and we never see your data.

    🎧 Audio safety

    Tones and pulses are generated live. Loud or sustained sound can be uncomfortable or harmful to hearing — begin at a low volume, take breaks, and be cautious with headphones. If you have a seizure disorder, note that rhythmic pulsing light or sound can be a trigger; stop if you feel unwell.
