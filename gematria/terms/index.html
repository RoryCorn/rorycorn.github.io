Disclaimer (please read first)

Gematria Desk turns letters and dates into numbers using traditional ciphers, then shows where those numbers match. It is offered for entertainment, education and curiosity.

  • Not financial advice. Nothing in Gematria Desk is financial, investment, trading or tax advice. Nothing in it is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold gold, silver, stocks, crypto or anything else. Rory Corn is not a licensed financial adviser or broker.
  • Not betting advice. Nothing here is a tip on any game or wager.
  • Gematria Desk makes no predictions. When these number rules were tested on ten years of gold, silver, S&P 500 and Bitcoin prices and eleven NFL seasons, they did no better than chance.
  • Matches happen by chance all the time. With many ciphers and many ways to add up a date, almost any two names or dates will share a number. That is why every connection comes with a chance check: how often random look-alike names and dates match just as well.
  • Fact and belief are kept apart. Each number’s meaning is labelled Math (worked out), Science (such as atomic numbers), Record (a sourced fact) or Lore (what some traditions believe). Lore describes belief. It is not proof of anything.
  • Dates and records can contain mistakes. Dates and records are checked against their sources but can contain mistakes. Hebrew dates shown are the daytime date (a Hebrew day begins at sunset the evening before). 13-month dates follow Eastern Time.
  • Respect for tradition. Gematria comes from Jewish tradition, and Greek isopsephy is a close relative. They appear here as history and number play, not as religious teaching.
