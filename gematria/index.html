How it works

Gematria turns letters into numbers; isopsephy does the same with Greek letters, counting each letter as a number. This part, like Privacy and terms, is open to everyone.

1. Letters become numbers

A cipher gives each letter a number. In English Ordinal, A=1, B=2 ... Z=26. “Gold” = G7 + O15 + L12 + D4 = 38. The four core ciphers are English Ordinal, Full Reduction (each letter shrunk to one digit), Reverse Ordinal (Z=1 ... A=26) and Reverse Full Reduction. Eight more are in Every cipher.

Reduction adds a number’s digits until one digit is left: O is 15, and 1 + 5 = 6. Reverse Full Reduction does both: count back from Z, then reduce (G is 20 from Z, and 2 + 0 = 2).

Every letter, four ways

2. Dates become numbers

A date’s parts are added several ways. November 1, 2026: 11 + 1 + 26 = 38, 11 + 1 + 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 22, day 305 of the year, 60 days left, and the Hebrew date 21 Heshvan 5787. On the 13-Month Sky Calendar the same day is Scorpius 3 (month 9, day 3), which adds up its own way: 9 + 3 = 12.

3. A match is a shared number

When a name and a date give the same number, decoders call it a match. Gold = 38, and November 1, 2026 gives 38 (11 + 1 + 26), so decoders would say gold “matches” that day. The gap between two dates can match too, counted in days, weeks or months.

Decoders are people who look for meaning in these numbers.

4. Always check chance

Twelve ciphers times ten date sums make dozens of numbers, so matches are everywhere. “Gold” matches about a third of all days. Every result here says how often luck alone does the same. A match that luck gives 30% of the time is ordinary. One luck gives under 1% of the time is rare.

5. Meaning comes in layers

Math (prime, square, triangular) is always true. Science: gold is element 79, silver 47. Record: a Bible verse, law or market event, with its source. Lore: what decoders believe, marked as belief.

6. Tested, and no edge

When these number rules were tested on ten years of gold, silver, S&P 500 and Bitcoin prices and eleven NFL seasons, they did no better than chance. So nothing here is a forecast, and none of it is financial or betting advice.
