About the station

Kilocycle Radio is Rory Corn's station, and everything on it, from the music to the studio, was built from scratch. The name comes from early radio, when dials were marked in kilocycles before anyone said kilohertz.

Mixed like a DJ

Every song is measured for tempo, key and energy. The next song is picked to fit the last one, and the two are blended: a long mix with a bass swap when they match, a smooth crossfade when they don't.

Live, almost instantly

Hosts broadcast from a phone or a computer, and listeners hear them about two seconds later instead of the usual ten to fifteen.

A studio in the browser

The host's console has an equalizer, compressor, voice changer, sound pads, phone lines for callers and a profanity delay, all in a web page.
