AI & voice

I use AI every day, to build faster and as the product itself.

Conversational voice agents

Shifty and Black at it! are live, paid apps built on ElevenLabs conversational agents, with Node.js servers and Apple in-app purchase verification. Getting a voice agent to behave in the real world is its own craft. For example:

  • When background noise kept cutting Black at it! off mid-sentence, I traced the cause to its turn-taking settings rather than its prompt, and fixed it there.
  • Black at it! picks a conversation back up where it left off after the app is backgrounded or the connection drops.
  • Shifty speaks in a voice cloned from mine.

About

I’m Rory Corn. I design and build software on my own: iOS apps, browser games, voice AI and the servers behind them. I handle the whole path to launch: code, 3D models, sound, store listings, screenshots, promo videos and App Review. Since 2017 I’ve also run Getm12, where I release my original music and art.

My professional background is 20+ years in public transit operations: supervising a 24/7 operation, coaching frontline teams, and solving problems in real time. That shapes how I build: reliable, clear, and made for real people.
